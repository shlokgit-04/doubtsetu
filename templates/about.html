About DoubtSetu

DoubtSetu is a study buddy for class 6-12 students in India: snap or type a doubt, and get the concept, a step-by-step solve, a practice example and a 3-question quiz — in English, Hindi or Marathi.

How it works

  • You type or photograph your doubt; the text goes to Google's Gemini model with a tutor prompt.
  • The model returns a structured answer (concept, steps, example, quiz), which the app renders.
  • Guests get 3 doubts a day; a free account gets 20. Accounts keep your email and a bcrypt-hashed password, nothing more.

Built for

STEM2Connect AI Hackathon 2026. Stack: Python Flask, SQLite, bcrypt sessions, Gemini API, gunicorn on Render.
